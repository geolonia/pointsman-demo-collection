Pointsmancollection demo

Bulky waste, collected on a day that fits

Book a bulky waste collection in Nishinomiya or Ashiya, step by step. The items and fees are the cities' own lists, kept in GeonicDB, a FIWARE data platform. Pointsman checks the booking, and picks a day that fits the collection truck's route, with a reason for each.

A demo: nothing goes to the cities, no truck comes, and the booking is deleted after a day. The route costs are a simple model for now, not real routes. Book with your city for a real collection.
